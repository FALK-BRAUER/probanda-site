How we measure reliability

Production starts at Governed.

We use Agent Reliability Engineering (ARE) levels to say where each agent stands. The weakest critical area sets the level.

Level 1 · experiment
Unmanaged

Shared credentials, rules in the prompt. No answer to "which agent did this?"

Level 2 · pilot
Observed

Own identity, policy on paper, a person pulls the plug.

Level 3 · production
Governed

Limits enforced outside the agent, a full trail, automatic halt. Where we take you.

Level 4 · scale
Accountable

Authority traced through sub-agents. Autonomy gained and lost on measured reliability. Where we go next.

Level 5
Adaptive

Controls tighten themselves after incidents.

Levels from the ARE Manifesto v1.0 (Hogan), a working draft, not a standard. Stage names and "production starts at Governed" are our view; we make no conformance claim.

Questions

Five questions for your next steering meeting.

  1. How many agents act in your systems today? Does each have its own identity and a named owner?
  2. Which actions run without a person approving them? And who decided that?
  3. If an agent made a wrong payment tomorrow, what could you show? What it saw, what it decided, who approved.
  4. Does your access design include agents? Own user, narrowest role, named owner.
  5. A vendor promises savings from agents? Ask for the method and the baseline.
