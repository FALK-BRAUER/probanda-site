How we measure reliability

Reliability you can measure. Proof you can check.

We use Agent Reliability Engineering (ARE) as a shared vocabulary. Each agent is scored on each reliability area, and the weakest critical one sets its level.

Level 1
Unmanaged

Agents and their authority undefined.

Level 2
Observed

Actions logged. Authority still broad.

Level 3 · pilot
Governed

Rules enforced. Approval steps live.

Level 4 · our target
Accountable

Every action traced to a person and a rule.

Level 5 · planned
Adaptive

Authority grows or shrinks on measured reliability.
