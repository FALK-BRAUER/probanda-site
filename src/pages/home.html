What is at stake when an agent gets it wrong
Money

A wrong payment, refund or posting is a direct loss, and an audit finding.

Accountability

Your board answers for what the agent did, not the vendor. The agent's own log is no proof: the system being checked wrote it.

Regulation

The EU AI Act asks for records and human oversight of high-risk AI. Singapore's agentic AI framework asks the same of every agent.
