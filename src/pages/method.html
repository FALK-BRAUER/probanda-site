Ten ARE controls

What Probanda does for each one.

01
Identity

Read from your identity platform on every call. Unknown agent or tool = deny.

02
Authority

Signed mandates. Sub-agents narrower than their parent. Only Probanda holds the write key.

03
Contracts

What the agent declared is checked against what ran, by independent witnesses.

04
Policy

Your rulebook, signed, two-person activation.

05
Evaluation

Test sets per agent in your testing tools. Rules tested on your past cases.

06
Testing

A release check on every agent, model or rule change: repeat tests, attack tests, tests on past records.

07
Runtime controls

Fails closed: deny, escalate, kill switch.

08
Observability

Every run traced in your monitoring tools. Targets for success rate and speed. Completeness checked.

09
Governance

Control mapping and evidence packs. Status to your risk and compliance system.

10
Continuous improvement

Reliability scorecard per agent. Drift alerts. Promotion evidence (planned).
