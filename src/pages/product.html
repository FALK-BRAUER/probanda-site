Autonomy

Each agent earns its freedom, step by step.

Evidence moves an agent up. Drift, a breach or a model change moves it back. Loosening a rule needs a test on past cases and two signatures.

1
Suggests

The agent proposes. A person acts.

2
Each step approved

The agent acts after a named person says yes.

3
Within limits

Routine actions run alone. Risky ones go to a person.

4
Alone

Earned on evidence, per action. Taken back on drift.

Steps follow progressive autonomy (ARE, draft). They map to MLCommons ARP tiers T0, T2, T3 and T4.

Frameworks

How we use each framework.

Probanda turns these frameworks into controls that run every day, with the evidence to show it. Your organisation stays responsible for its own assessment.

ARE (Agent Reliability Engineering)

An open working draft (Hogan, v1.0): ten controls and five maturity levels for agents.

What we do. We score each agent against it in the readiness check, and use it to decide when an agent earns more autonomy, and when it loses it.

Not claimed: ARE is a draft, not a standard. We make no conformance claim.

IMDA Agentic AI framework

Singapore's Model AI Governance Framework for Agentic AI. Four dimensions: bound the risks upfront, keep humans accountable, technical controls, end-user responsibility.

What we do. We set each agent's limits before it acts, send risky actions to a named person, run the checks and the kill switch, and keep records outside the agent's control, as IMDA's agentic testing kit asks.

Not claimed: Endorsement by IMDA.

AI Verify

IMDA's testing framework, with process checks across 11 principles.

What we do. Our evidence is tagged with the AI Verify process checks it supports, so your testers, including outside testers, can use it directly.

Not claimed: AI Verify is a testing framework, not a certificate.

ISO/IEC 42001

The international standard for AI management systems.

What we do. We produce the evidence for its operating controls: event logs, monitoring and human oversight. Our evidence is also tagged with ISO/IEC 42001 controls, using AI Verify's published mapping, so one set of evidence serves both.

Not claimed: Your organisation is certified, by an accredited body. Probanda is not.

EU AI Act

EU law. Where an agent's use is high-risk, it asks for automatic records (Art. 12), human oversight (Art. 14) and logs kept by the deployer (Art. 26).

What we do. We record every action, let a person step in, and keep the records in your environment for as long as you set.

Not claimed: "Compliant". Our evidence supports your own assessment.

MLCommons ARP (draft)

A draft profile for each agent deployment: autonomy, operating domain, actions and controls.

What we do. We fill each profile with tested evidence and keep it valid as you run. See below ↓

Not claimed: Endorsement by MLCommons. Your organisation owns and signs each profile.
