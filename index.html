Why this can't wait

The rules are here.

The EU AI Act, Korea's AI Basic Act and Singapore's agentic AI framework all ask for records and human oversight of AI. More follow by 2027.5

One error, a public problem.

An agent that pays the wrong supplier or leaks data does it at machine speed, and you answer for it, not the vendor.6

It never stops.

Models and tools change every few weeks or even days, and each change can change how an agent behaves.7

Everyone asks for the same controls.

Regulators and industry bodies agree: know your agents, a person on risky actions, a way to stop them, records that hold up. That is what Probanda is built to do.8
