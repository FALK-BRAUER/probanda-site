Who we are

Trust comes first

AI agents can make everyone more productive. But a company will only let an agent act once it trusts it the way it trusts an employee: clear limits, someone who answers for it, and a record of what it did.

We build that trust, so agents work alongside people and help them do more. A win for business and for society.

What we stand for

Proof, not promises.

Our name

Probanda is Latin for "things that must be proved". That is our job: every action an agent takes is checked, recorded and provable to your board, your auditor and your regulator.

We build on what you have

Your cloud and platforms already contain many of the parts. We connect them and add the proof. We don't reinvent the wheel.

It is never one-off

AI models and tools now change in weeks, sometimes days. Your controls have to keep pace, so we keep them current.
